# 🗺️ Roadmap

A roadmap is a realistic, flexible, up-to-date plan that provides structure for delivery and aligns with stakeholder requirements*.* It should communicate clear, measurable goals and outline the likely flow of project deliverables, with regular updates to adapt to changing business needs and lessons learned. The purpose is to provide stakeholders with a **high-level summary** of the project vision and lifecycle.

**Success Criteria:** A clear, realistic roadmap exists, agreed with stakeholders, and updated as the project evolves.

- **RED:** There is no realistic plan.
- **AMBER:** Plan in place but incomplete, inflexible, out-of-date or not clearly aligned with expectations and/or current capacity to deliver.
- **GREEN:** Roadmap realistic, agreed, and updated regularly with a trusted forward-look of milestones / key deliverables. The plan is flexible and changes are systematically agreed and recorded.
- **NA:** Only for small exploratory tasks, or where assignment is BAU or provision of ad-hoc advice/support rather than project delivery.

## Good Practice Guidance

| MSC    | Good Practice                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| MUST   | \* There is a baseline plan for delivering user requirements shared with stakeholders at start of the project.                                                                                                                             |
| MUST   | \* There is a culture of healthy challenge between customer and delivery team to ensure expectations around delivery times / resource requirements are realistic.                                                                          |
| SHOULD | \* The roadmap and timelines are amended where necessary, and these changes are recorded and communicated to ensure that all key stakeholders are aligned on the delivery timeline                                                         |
| SHOULD | \* Roadmap is kept in a readily shareable visual form (e.g. 'plan on a page' gantt chart) for easy sharing with stakeholders.                                                                                                              |
| SHOULD | \* Keep it high level - use Jira or other kanban / agile tool for task level project management. The roadmap is primarily for stakeholder alignment, keeping an eye on the big picture and supporting Business Planning Cycle discussions. |
| COULD  | \* Utilise point estimation and burn-down charts in Jira to keep on top of team velocity and update delivery timelines accordingly, aiming for increasing accuracy of delivery forecasts.                                                  |
