# Role Descriptions Guide

| Role | Mandatory / Optional | Role Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Commissioner | Mandatory | This is generally the senior customer that commissioned the work, although the role may transfer to another individual during the lifecycle of a product. They are unlikely to be an analyst, and may not have a detailed understanding of the analytical approach / modelling method used. They are accountable for the subsequent use of the model / product for decision making, and in doing so need to be assured of the quality of the product and aware of any residual risks / uncertainties.  **Must:**   * Agree scope of deliverables * Communicate context of the question being asked or problem to be addressed to the Lead Analyst and Analytical Assurer (so that likely risks and business criticality are understood). * Take account of quality statements / caveats / residual risks communicated by the Lead Analyst and / or Analytical Assurer when using the product to support decision making * Be accountable for use of the product by others under their authority. * Provide and/or permit sufficient time and resources so that the necessary level of quality assurance can be completed   **Should:**   * Work with the Lead Analyst to define the problem, criticality, scope and deliverables. * Participate in regular show & tells of progress and ask questions to clarify understanding of quality of outputs and understand the strengths and limitations and contexts of the analysis so that results are correctly interpreted. * Be available and/or facilitate access to stakeholders to provide subject matter expertise on the problem being tackled and wider context during project development. * Champion and sponsor working transparently and openly, and sharing of knowledge with wider projects. * Assist with resource allocation, including SMEs, whilst ensuring sufficient time and resources for the required level of assurance. * Assist with identifying dependencies with related work, products and systems |
| Lead Analyst | Mandatory | They are the analytical lead of the team responsible for delivering the data product or service. They convert the commission into an analytical plan and determine the analytical approach.  **Must:**   * Work with the Commissioner to define the problem, criticality, scope and deliverables * Validate the problem statement and user requirement. * Use resources available to deliver the analytical product, ensuring quality approaches throughout. * Identify and manage analytical risks * Present results and conclusion of the analysis to the commissioner and stakeholders and / or liaise with other partners to ensure delivery of the data product. * Make an assessment of what expertise and resources are required to deliver the agreed scope and deliverables at the required quality level, and share this assessment with Senior Management (the Senior Responsible Analyst). * Take account of assigned resource and available expertise to negotiate any required changes to scope and/or ensure that attendant quality risks are documented and appropriately shared. * Regularly engage with the Senior Responsible Analyst to help them stay abreast of main developments, risks and issues in the project, and to enable them to report on and champion the work in relevant settings - including arranging a review meeting at least once-a-quarter. * Ensure that an Analytical Assurer is appointed to the project * Ensure that all assurance activities are properly documented and communicated. * Ensure that any residual uncertainties and/or risks are documented and appropriately shared. * Invite Analytical Assurer to scrutinise the Quality Assurance processes employed for the project at appropriate times during project delivery - at a minimum once each quarter. * Arrange and prepare for a final AQA review from the Analytical Assurer in advance of handover of final product / analytical findings to customer. * Ensure that mandatory project documentation pages on confluence are kept up to date.   **Should:**   * Use the Quality Framework templates on confluence where relevant. * Capture any learning from experience throughout the project and share these with wider data science team (for example, feeding suggestions to Quality Processes team to update guidance). |
| Analytical Assurer | Mandatory | They are a senior analyst outside of the immediate analytical delivery team. The analytical assurer maintains a degree of independence from the day-to-day production of the analysis, allowing for objective review.  They are accountable for the overall analytical quality of the project, and they are responsible for ensuring that the Commissioner is made aware of any residual risks / uncertainties. They are not typically responsible for directly carrying out QA activity (such as verification and validation, code review, or comparison to other analysis), rather they are responsible for ensuring that the level of QA within the project is appropriate to the decision(s) being supported.  For projects of particular significance or complexity, an optional *Second Assurer* may be appointed.  **Must:**   * Receive evidence of appropriate Quality Assurance activity throughout the lifecycle of the project. * Scrutinise and challenge this evidence – through review of QA logs and asking probing questions. * Advise the commissioner whether appropriate QA has taken place * Provide final analytical ‘sign off’ of the product before its use by the Commissioner, confirming:   + That appropriate QA has been undertaken   + That the model / product is fit for purpose   + That residual risks, uncertainties and key assumptions have been clearly explained to the commissioner, and that they are aware of the need and equipped to account for these during use of the product. The Analytical Assurer is responsible for securing joint sign-off from the Commissioner to this effect.   **Should:**   * Make themselves available to attend QA review meetings as arranged by the Lead Analyst * Provide guidance to the Lead Analyst on the types of QA activity that will be required given the level of business criticality and/or uncertainties within a project. * Work collaboratively with the Lead Analyst to oversee and contribute to the continuous quality assurance process, including the regular review and revision of the Quality Assurance Framework (QaF) log. * Share lessons from other projects and/or wider experience that could enhance quality of this work |
| Senior Responsible Analyst | Mandatory | This is a senior (generally 8C or above) member of staff who provides strategic oversight to the project, and can be a senior contact point for the Commissioner if required.  **Must:**   * Support the Lead Analyst to enable them to deliver the agreed project scope to high quality. This may include:   + Providing mentorship / advice on analytical approach   + Supporting in securing necessary resources to deliver the project   + Supporting in negotiating scope with the Commissioner * Support escalating issues for resolution, where project has dependencies beyond immediate team * Act as initial escalation point for any concerns raised by the Commissioner or any other partners / stakeholders * Support the team in meetings / presentations where senior representation would be helpful. * Be accountable for the accuracy of status reporting on the project for upward reporting (e.g. Book of Work, Advanced Analytics SLT)   **Should:**   * Participate is a one-a-quarter review of the project, with the project lead plus any other team members required, in order to:   + stay abreast of main developments, risks and issues.   + act as a sounding board and guide for key decision points in the project. * Represent the team and project requirements during quarterly Business Planning Cycle discussions. * Champion the project and the team in wider settings. * Participate in regular team retrospectives. |
| 2nd Assurer | Optional | For projects of particular significance or complexity, an optional Second Assurer may be appointed by the Analytical Assurer.  The Analytical Assurer and 2nd Assurer may work together to discharge the duties of the Analytical Assurer role – potentially dividing up responsibilities for a project by expertise in particular techniques, or in any other way convenient to the project scope.  They may simply provide additional scrutiny across all aspects of the project – being a ‘second opinion’ or ‘second pair of eyes’ to the Analytical Assurer.  The Analytical Assurer remains accountable for providing final analytical sign-off, drawing on recommendations from the 2nd assurer. |
| Quality Lead | Optional | This optional role may be assigned by the Lead Analyst to another member of staff in the project team. They will act as a guide and advocate of quality processes throughout delivery. The position might rotate around the team every few months to allow all members to embed quality processes and foster wider understanding.  The Quality Lead supports the Lead Analyst in discharging many of the AQA responsibilities in the project, but accountability for overall compliance still remains with the Lead Analyst (per the duties listed above).  **Could:**   * Take the lead on ensuring completion of Quality Process Framework templates for the project. * Carry out ‘Confluence gardening’ and ensure the analytical teams keep confluence pages updated with activities and evidence. * Establish and monitor local processes to assure quality (such as arrangements for peer review / code review or establishing quality criteria for moving tasks to ‘Done’). * Run retrospectives focused on Quality Processes and adherence with the Quality Processes Framework. * Check in with the analytical team on a regular basis, helping them ensure that the project is following the agreed level of assurance. * Identify any lessons that could be shared amount the wider Data Science community or improvements to the framework and through periodical meetings or discussions with other quality leads. * Share learning with other project teams and the Quality Processes function team |
