# DS_xxx: Scope

The main purpose of this page to establish a shared understanding between commissioner and delivery team on the scope of what is to be delivered. And what won't be.

Having a clear [📃 Agreed Commission](../%F0%9F%93%83%20Agreed%20Commission.md) with specific [📋 User Requirements](../%F0%9F%93%8B%20User%20Requirements.md) is fundamental to delivering a [quality data science](../DS%20Quality%20Framework.md) project.

The detail of requirements will be fleshed out during delivery and tracked via JIRA tasks and other Confluence pages.

Use this page to **establish a baseline**, and to document any **agreed major changes** to scope.

Include any original commissioning documents / NWRs in this page.

## Overarching objective(s)

Guidance:

What is the overall driving purpose of this project - what does it need to deliver, for who, and why?

Most projects will have just one overall objective - having the focus of a single goal is helpful.  But some projects might have additional key customers with distinct requirements.

You might formulate this as a top level [User Story](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/agile-delivery/writing-user-stories).

**As a** ...

**I need** ...

**So that** ...

## Specific requirements

Guidance

The [MoSCoW method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MoSCoW_method) is a light touch way to clarify and prioritise requirements.  We will generally look to time-box delivery of phases of projects within the Business Planning Cycle. So be disciplined in restricting 'Must' deliverables to those we have confidence in delivering within the current time box.
