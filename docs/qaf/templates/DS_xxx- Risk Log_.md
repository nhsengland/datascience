# DS_xxx: Risk Log_

This log is used to maintain an ongoing record of Risks and Issues that emerge during the project.

All projects encounter risks to delivering their objectives - and those risks may become issues as they materialise.  For example, where a project depends on access to external inputs (datasets, platforms, approvals or third-party delivery), these should be recorded as risks where there is uncertainty or potential impact to delivery. A full guidance can be found here: Project Risks Guide

By identifying risks and issues, and ensuring we have a plan to deal with them, we can improve chances of successful delivery.

[⚠️ Risk Management](../%E2%9A%A0%EF%B8%8F%20Risk%20Management.md) is an essential part of delivering a [quality data science](../DS%20Quality%20Framework.md) project.

Maintaining a risk log is **mandatory for all projects**, to support consistency, oversight and effective quality assurance. The level of detail and frequency of review should be proportionate to the project, with expectations set by the assigned Quality Assurer.
