# DS*xxx: Assumptions Log*

This Assumptions Log is used to maintain an ongoing record of assumptions that are made as the project progresses.

We rarely have perfect information when undertaking a data science project. So making simplifying and/or pragmatic assumptions is essential to enable projects to progress.

In fact, identifying, clarifying and testing assumptions is at the heart of delivering any analytical work.

Maintaining a complete and up-to-date assumptions log is an [essential part](../%F0%9F%92%AD%20Assumptions.md) of delivering a [quality data science](../DS%20Quality%20Framework.md) project.
