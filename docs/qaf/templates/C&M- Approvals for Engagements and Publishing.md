# Guidelines on publishing content on our public facing pathways

Before publishing anything, best to think about if this is something we should publish and whether you need approvals.

So here are some brief guidelines on when to ask for approval and where to go.

#F5F5F5#FFAA98In-house help

**Before embarking seeking approval outside the team**

* Read the guidance [NHS England social media team](https://nhsengland.sharepoint.com/sites/thehub/SitePages/Social-Media.aspx "https://nhsengland.sharepoint.com/sites/thehub/SitePages/Social-Media.aspx")
* Talk to our **Comms & Marketing Function team** for advice
* **Raise it with your manager** (where it could filter up to the senior leadership of the data science team)

The exact criteria still aren't clear, however some things to consider:

* **The comms team are not concerned with technical content (e.g. documentation)**Click to know more about this...

  There are multiple levels of comms teams that we've attempted to engage with around publicising of our work. However, we have found that these teams are more interested in high level communications rather than technical dissemination and previous attempts to promote our technical work have been poorly received and burdensome to progress.  These teams quite rightly control the NHS branding quite severely (as this is a trusted brand which they don't want damaged) and the social media posts from NHS England.  However, **they are not concerned with materials being published through github or academic journals as these are technical details aimed at specific audiences**.

  The recommended approach is to closely consider the content going up on our public github repos and then use the io sites for explaining and signposting this content.  This moves the question of release to why the content is on github in the first place which is easily defendable around open collaboration.   This also means the branding can remain on the site as the content is NHS England github and we are an NHS England Team so perfectly reasonable to use the logo as no new content here.
* Make sure there is **no embargo on sharing your content** (e.g. sensitive work, unreleased policy, purdah during elections)
* If you're **running an event that has an expense**, then **contact comms** to get assistant in **ensuring maximum impact**
* **Social Media**: Make sure you view the [central social media guidance](https://nhsengland.sharepoint.com/sites/thehub/SitePages/Social-Media.aspx)  if you want to post about our work. Also, check out [C&M: Use of Social Media](./C%26M-%20Use%20of%20Social%20Media.md)

# Routes: where and how to publish

The followings are some examples of what we can publish and a suitable home for that content.

#F5F5F5#A9A9A9Github Repos

* **code**, with appropriate licenses, documentation etc...
* the **readme should focus on code usage**
* **other documentation should go into a technical report** or io page as a user guide
* **only fake data,** signed off public results and public data sits alongside the code
#F5F5F5#A9A9A9Academic Articles

* novel **methodology**
* **results** signed-off for release by data owner
#F5F5F5#A9A9A9Data Science Site

[NHS England Data Science](https://nhsengland.github.io/datascience/)

* **Technical** (generally, **content should already be published on GitHub** - the io site is then just a shop window)
* **What we are doing and our interests** (staying away from anything too opinionated where possible)
* **Case studies of our projects and articles/blogs** where **all content is already published elsewhere**
* **Signposts** to published materials

Before you publish to a public repo, such as adding a page on our website, get the content reviewed to ensure it's all above board. [C&M: Website Gateway](https://nhsd-confluence.digital.nhs.uk/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=819725734)

#F5F5F5#A9A9A9NHS England and Transformation Sites

* **Full Cases studies** detailing theneed, approach, results and impact
* **Policy**
#F5F5F5#A9A9A9AnalystX Site

* **Community materials**
* **Engagement asks** (e.g. anything asking people to get involved)
