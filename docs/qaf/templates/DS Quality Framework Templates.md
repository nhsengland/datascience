# Templates

Welcome to the Templates section of our quality assurance framework, they are designed to support consistent quality assurance practices.

> **Note on Formatting:** These templates were originally created for our internal knowledge management and collaboration workspace (Atlassian Confluence). We are sharing them here so that others can make use of the underlying structure, prompts, and content. 
>
> Because they have been exported from Confluence, we recognise that the formatting may not translate cleanly across every platform. Please feel free to adapt, reformat, and tailor these templates to suit whichever documentation tools or workflows your team uses.
