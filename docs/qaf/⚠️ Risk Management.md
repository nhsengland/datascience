# ⚠️ Risk Management

_Risks and issues must be identified, managed, and communicated to avoid surprises and ensure informed decision-making._

**Success Criteria:** Risks and issues are systematically identified, recorded, monitored, and managed.

- **RED:** No risk management evident.
- **AMBER:** Risks acknowledged but not consistently monitored or managed. Risk capture may not be comprehensive.
- **GREEN:** Risk and issues actively tracked with effective strategies in place.

## Good Practice Guidance

| MSC    | Good Practice                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| MUST   | \* There are mechanisms to identify, manage and report risks and issues that may impact either the project itself, or the wider organisation.                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| MUST   | \* There is a culture that encourages early identification and reporting on risks.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| MUST   | \* Risks with wider impacts that cannot be controlled within the project are escalated to SMT promptly.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| MUST   | \* Maintain a risk register and issues log for the project.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| MUST   | \* Contact someone from [Portfolio & Compliance Function Team](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/channel/19%3A92ebe82593424ee0b3f631573b5d531d%40thread.tacv2/FT%20-%20Portfolio%20and%20Compliance?groupId=f20fa1cf-df47-400e-83f1-091385118378) to review your risks / facilitate a risk workshop at least once during project lifecycle. |
| SHOULD | \* Ensure risks have appropriate strategies assigned                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| SHOULD | \* Ensure any required actions have been captured in a backlog, e.g. as a Jira ticket that is linked to from the Confluence template                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
