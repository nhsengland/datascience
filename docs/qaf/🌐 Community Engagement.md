# 🌐 Community Engagement

_Engaging with relevant communities enhances quality, shares learning, and builds networks of expertise._

**Success Criteria:** Project engages constructively with relevant communities, drawing on and contributing to shared practice.

- **RED:** Little to no engagement with external experts or leaders in the field.
- **AMBER:** Limited collaboration or engagement.
- **GREEN:** Regular and effective engagement with leaders in the field leading to improved quality and collaboration.

## Good Practice Guidance

Projects of high maturity will benefit from discussion with other leaders within the field to compare techniques and analysis to improve our internal processes through collaboration. By identifying and contributing to relevant networks we can amplify the impact of our work and enhance our reputation with potential partners.

| MSC    | Good Practice                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| SHOULD | _ Relevant networks,  communities of practice and/or centres of excellence have been identified, and considered for potential for collaboration / engagement. _ Engagement could simply be seeking to learn from their published good practice. \* Consider engaging with communities that would be interested in or are doing similar work to your project. |
| SHOULD | \* Plan developed - with clarity on the level and mode of engagement for target networks.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| COULD  | \* Active participation in network activities, pro-active sharing of learning from this project with network.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |

### Examples

Some examples of communities could be:

- National data strategy (<https://www.gov.uk/guidance/national-data-strategy>) and data leaders network (<https://www.gov.uk/government/groups/data-advisory-board-and-data-leaders-network>)
- [ONS Quality Champions network](https://analysisfunction.civilservice.gov.uk/government-statistical-service-and-statistician-group/champion-networks/quality-champions/) to share best practices and lessons learnt on quality across the organisation
- Experts such as [Methodology](https://www.ons.gov.uk/methodology/methodologytopicsandstatisticalconcepts/howwecreateouroutputs), the [Data Science Campus](https://datasciencecampus.ons.gov.uk/) and academia
- [GSS RAP Champions network](https://analysisfunction.civilservice.gov.uk/support/reproducible-analytical-pipelines/reproducible-analytical-pipeline-rap-champions/)
- [Data Linkage Champion Network](https://analysisfunction.civilservice.gov.uk/support/data-linkage/data-linkage-champion-network/)
