*Identifying and engaging the commissioner and stakeholders ensures that scope, expectations, and needs are clear from the outset and through delivery.*

**Success Criteria:** Roles of commissioner and stakeholders are clear, documented, and supported by a plan for engagement.

* **RED:** No clear understanding or agreement on commissioner or stakeholders.
* **AMBER:** Some understanding and agreement on the roles of stakeholders and the commissioner but no plan for engagement. Contact and input from SMEs is infrequent / irregular, potentially leading to misunderstandings.
* **GREEN:** Stakeholders or commissioners are clear on their role. A comprehensive plan for stakeholder engagement is in place, and there is regular and constructive engagement with relevant SMEs.

## Good Practice Guidance

| MSC | Good Practice |
| --- | --- |
| MUST | * Identify the commissioner and document their role and what input the project can expect from them, taking into account seniority, time-availability etc. * Ensure that there is clear commissioning ownership throughout the project - including through organisational change, staff moves and/or changes in project priorities. |
| MUST | * Key stakeholders and their roles and interest in the project have been agreed upon. |
| SHOULD | * There is a plan for how stakeholders will be involved through project delivery. |
| SHOULD | * Conduct reviews of your stakeholders to ensure new stakeholders are identified as the project progresses |
| SHOULD | * There is suitable engagement with relevant SMEs (data, user need, clinical) as relevant throughout the project |
| SHOULD | * Identify relevant customers for your project    + Categorise the users of the analytical output into groups according to their type of use and data needs.   + Determine a hierarchy of customers, and which groups' needs should be prioritised.   + Maintain a continuous dialogue to update customers on key project milestones and allow for regular reviews that the product meets their desired requirements. |

See the [Role Descriptions Guide](https://nhsd-confluence.digital.nhs.uk/spaces/DAT/pages/461265169/Role+Descriptions+Guide) for a detailed explanation of a commissioner and other roles.
