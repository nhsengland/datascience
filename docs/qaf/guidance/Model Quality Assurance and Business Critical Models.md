# Model Quality Assurance and Business Critical Models

# Overview

Quality assurance of models is the complete process of ensuring:

1. The nature and quality of evidence is
   1. Fit for purpose
   2. Sufficient to support the stated aims and objectives
2. It is clear who is responsible for the model
3. Comprehensive and clear documentation exists
4. Where appropriate, there are agreed change control processes in place

NHS England aims to provide a framework of QA to manage and reduce the risk of error in business critical models, in line with the recommendations of the [Macpherson review](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/review-of-quality-assurance-of-government-models).

The content below briefly summarises the process of model QA in NHS England. We recommend going to the official source and take the following as a nice summary.

# Formal QA processes in NHSE

## How to decide if a model is business critical?

The Macpherson review asks departments to consider the extent to which

1. Outputs drive key financial and funding decisions
2. Outputs essential to the achievement of their business plan
3. Errors could lead to serious financial, legal or reputational damage

## Business Critical Model QA Review Process

As outlined on [this page](https://nhs.sharepoint.com/sites/ModelQualityAssurance/SitePages/QA.aspx?xsdata=MDV8MDJ8bWF0dGhldy50YXlsb3I1N0BuaHMubmV0fGIyMzAzMDVkMTM2NDRkNGIxMzAxMDhkZDBhZmE1ZWYwfDM3YzM1NGIyODViMDQ3ZjViMjIyMDdiNDhkNzc0ZWUzfDB8MHw2Mzg2Nzg3OTI3Mjc2NjEzODl8VW5rbm93bnxUV0ZwYkdac2IzZDhleUpGYlhCMGVVMWhjR2tpT25SeWRXVXNJbFlpT2lJd0xqQXVNREF3TUNJc0lsQWlPaUpYYVc0ek1pSXNJa0ZPSWpvaVRXRnBiQ0lzSWxkVUlqb3lmUT09fDB8fHw%3D&sdata=eUZtbDI0emVicmZiY0NVWS8rUVhGcmkxbkZKZUtUQzRvZnlHTUN5V3Nxbz0%3D&CT=1733322749749&OR=OWA-NT-Mail&CID=2baa067c-7065-bc7b-43cc-951b5cbda00d&clickParams=eyJYLUFwcE5hbWUiOiJNaWNyb3NvZnQgT3V0bG9vayBXZWIgQXBwIiwiWC1BcHBWZXJzaW9uIjoiMjAyNDExMTUwMDMuMjciLCJPUyI6IldpbmRvd3MgMTEifQ%3D%3D), the QA review process for models (and significant analysis) in NHSE is:

1. Submit your model or significant analysis to the register using [this form](https://nhs.sharepoint.com/sites/ModelQualityAssurance/Lists/Register%20of%20Significant%20Analysis%20%20Models%20202122/NewForm.aspx) - leave the triage and QA inputs blank for now, this can be filled in later
2. Complete the [triage template](https://nhs.sharepoint.com/:x:/r/sites/ModelQualityAssurance/Shared/NHSE%20QA%20Business%20Critical%20Triage.xlsx) to self-assess whether your model or piece of significant analysis is business critical
3. Submit completed triage template to [england.businesscriticalmodelling@nhs.net](mailto:england.businesscriticalmodelling@nhs.net) and update your row on [the register](https://nhs.sharepoint.com/sites/ModelQualityAssurance/Lists/Register%20of%20Significant%20Analysis%20%20Models%20202122/AllItems.aspx) with your results from filling out the triage template
4. Triage template is reviewed by the business critical models team and a final decision made on it's criticality
5. If the model or analysis is deemed to be business critical then the team will ask you to complete the [Business Critical Model QA Review Framework](https://nhs.sharepoint.com/:x:/r/sites/ModelQualityAssurance/Shared/NHSE%20Model%20QA%20Template.xlsm) and also submit this to [england.businesscriticalmodelling@nhs.net](mailto:england.businesscriticalmodelling@nhs.net)
