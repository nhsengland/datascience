# 🙋‍♂️ Roles & Responsibilities

INLINE

*Clarity of project team roles ensures accountability and effective collaboration, reducing duplication or gaps in responsibility.*

**Success Criteria:** Roles and responsibilities are clearly defined, assigned, and agreed across the project team.

* **RED:** Roles or responsibilities for the project team have not been defined or agreed upon.
* **AMBER:** Roles and responsibilities for the project team have been partially defined and agreed upon, but there may still be some areas of ambiguity or outstanding roles that need to be clarified.
* **GREEN:** All roles and responsibilities for the project team have been clearly defined and agreed upon.

## Good Practice Guidance

| MSC | Good Practice |
| --- | --- |
| MUST | * Identify and assign the Lead Analyst and ensure their role is clearly defined in the context of the given project |
| MUST | * Assign an analytical assurer who is outside of the immediate project team (usually Rupert or Sarah). |
| MUST | * Identify and assign the Senior Responsible Analyst and agree how they will support the lead analyst and other team members (Usually an 8C data scientist). |
| SHOULD | * Record all other project members and get to know their skills and how they will contribute to the project |
| SHOULD | * Identify specialist roles that require Subject Matter Expertise (SME) early in the project. |
| COULD | * Assign optional roles such as Quality Lead (for larger projects and/or projects of greater complexity or business-criticality) |
