# 👀 Peer Review

INLINE

*Peer review provides independent challenge to methods, outputs, and code. It should be planned throughout the lifecycle, not just at the end.*

**Success Criteria:** Peer review is regular, systematic, and carried out by suitably qualified colleagues, with feedback documented and acted upon.

* **RED:** No peer review process or reviews rarely undertaken.
* **AMBER:** Occasional peer reviews conducted. Code quality checks exist but lack thoroughness.
* **GREEN:** Regular and systematic peer reviews conducted by qualified colleagues, with a culture of welcoming critical challenge.

## Good Practice Guidance

| MSC | Good Practice |
| --- | --- |
| MUST | * [Define the level](./%F0%9F%AA%9C%20Proportionate%20QA.md) and scope of peer review required. |
| MUST | * Apply peer review across the whole project - not just code review, but to validate [assumptions](./%F0%9F%92%AD%20Assumptions.md), secure critical review of [methodological choices](./%F0%9F%A7%AA%20Approach%20%26%20Methodology.md) and testing that the approach will meet user's need and intended purpose. **Validation** as well as **Verification**. |
| MUST | * Ensure reviewers are sufficiently qualified to review the work and provide valuable feedback. |
| MUST | * Establish a culture in the project that celebrates and welcomes Peer Review and constructive feedback, and ensure tasks to provide review and feedback are prioritised |
| SHOULD | * Have a clear 'Review' stage before tasks are moved to Done. |
| SHOULD | * For key business critical models or results obtain independent assurance, potentially outside NHSE. |
| SHOULD | * Record peer review feedback and actions taken as part of the project documentation for [transparency](./%F0%9F%94%8E%20Transparency.md). |
| COULD | * Utilise [transparency](./%F0%9F%94%8E%20Transparency.md) (e.g. GitHub publication) and [networking](./%F0%9F%8C%90%20Community%20Engagement.md) with relevant Communities of Interest to 'crowd source' peer review. |
