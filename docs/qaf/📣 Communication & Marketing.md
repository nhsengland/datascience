# 📣 Communication & Marketing

_Clear communication and active promotion ensure outputs are visible, understood, and used, maximising impact._

**Success Criteria:** Results and conclusions are communicated clearly, tailored to audiences, and supported by a communications plan.

- **RED:** Results and conclusions are unclear or confusing. No or minimal active marketing of the work. Presentation doesn't match the project type or audience's understanding level.
- **AMBER:** Results are largely clear, with only minor ambiguities. Target audience is not well defined and/or not fully reached. Presentation approach is not tailored to the audience.
- **GREEN:** Results communicated clearly, appropriately, and proactively promoted in line with a communications plan. The presentation method both aligns with the project type and is tailored to the audience.

## Good Practice Guidance

| MSC    | Good Practice                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| MUST   | \* **Manage active participation, feedback and collaboration** with internal and external stakeholders by ensuring that queries are recorded, and that responses are provided.                                                                   |
| SHOULD | \* Have clear channels of communication for users and other stakeholders, allowing for early identification of issues, misinterpretations, or drifting requirements + Respond to issues + Respond to PRs on code + Monitor inbox (if applicable) |
| MUST   | \* **Key Exploitable Results** (KERs) have been identified and a development plan has been discussed for their target audience.                                                                                                                  |
| MUST   | \* **Target Groups** have been identified once key exploitable results of the project have been defined.                                                                                                                                         |
| SHOULD | \* A **communication plan** exists (using the Marketing routes) which is tailored to the different levels of engagement.                                                                                                                         |
| SHOULD | \* Check the Marketing & Engagement toolkitfor more ideas and resources to facilitate this activity.                                                                                                                                             |
| SHOULD | \* Have at least one **internal**and one **external**facing promotion initiative (find out which one here Marketing routes)                                                                                                                      |
| MUST   | \* Any external promotion material is approved at the right level (e.g. project lead, C&M team). See C&M: Approvals for Engagements and Publishing                                                                                               |
| SHOULD | \* The**desired impact is stated** and possibly **tracked**. It can be generically measured as the positive actions generated from the campaign (e.g. other teams using our code/tools, more commissions for our team)                           |
