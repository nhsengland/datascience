# ⚖️ Ethics & Fairness

INLINE

*Ethical considerations ensure projects are fair, transparent, and responsible, avoiding bias or harm and supporting trust in outputs.*

**Success Criteria:** Ethical and fairness issues are actively identified, mitigated, and documented through appropriate assessments.

* **RED:** Ethical and fairness issues have not been actively considered.
* **AMBER:** Some ethical and fairness issues have been considered, but appropriate mitigations are not yet in place or fully implemented.
* **GREEN:** Ethical and fairness concerns have been documented, actively addressed and integrated into the project.

## Good Practice Guidance

| MSC | Good Practice |
| --- | --- |
| SHOULD | * Work through the resources on the [Ethics Hub](https://nhsd-confluence.digital.nhs.uk/spaces/DAT/pages/749482620/Ethics+Hub) page, covering:   + **Fairness** - How have you or how will you ensure that your project/tool/product/algorithm is accessible, equitable and inclusive?   + **Transparency** - Have you ensured that, or do you have a plan to make, your work visible and explainable?   + **Value-adding** - How have you or how will you ensure that your project/tool/product/algorithm adds value and has clear benefits whilst respecting human-autonomy and the environment?   + **Reliable** - Is your project/tool/product/algorithm accurate, consistent and reliable? |
| SHOULD | Complete an Ethics Statement using resources on the [Ethics Hub](https://nhsd-confluence.digital.nhs.uk/spaces/DAT/pages/749482620/Ethics+Hub) page |
| SHOULD | Complete a [Data Hazards Assessment](https://nhsd-confluence.digital.nhs.uk/spaces/DAT/pages/1066483409/Data+Hazards+-+How+to+run+an+assessment), and ensure any identified risks/issues are logged and acted upon where necessary. |
